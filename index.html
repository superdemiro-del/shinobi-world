Casting system

Jutsu use 2–5 timed hand seals and travel up to 6–8 tiles. Train control with C near the post. Movement or enemy attacks interrupt casting. Fire beats Wind, Wind beats Lightning, Lightning beats Earth, Earth beats Water, and Water beats Fire. Enemy affinity is Earth.

Heritage, clan arts & exploration

Clan arts cost chakra and have cooldowns. Travel resets the sparring encounter; your character and progress are saved.

Systems implemented

Two-parent ancestry, separate clan identity, inheritable training modifiers, chakra capacity variation, elemental affinity, potential dojutsu inheritance, combat training, academy missions, experience, ranks, unlockable jutsu and chakra consumption.

Rasengan & Jinchūriki

Rasengan is a charged close-range technique. Tailed beasts are optional experimental seals, not inherited bloodlines.

Controls: G Rasengan · V cycle unlocked cloak stages. Transformation drains beast chakra, and high-output stages cause strain.
